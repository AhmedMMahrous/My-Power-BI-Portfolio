## 🔍 Overview
An interactive and visually appealing Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Sales Performance across multiple dimensions such as Country, City, Product, and Sub-Category.
The dashboard enables real-time tracking of KPIs like Gross Profit, Total Revenue, and Total Units, comparing them against dynamic goals.

[![image.jpg](https://i.postimg.cc/ydzBFj1S/image.jpg)](https://postimg.cc/McthwyjW)
## ✨ Key Features
Dark-themed professional design for enhanced readability and a modern look.

Multiple filters (Year, Quarter, Country, Town) for easy drill-down analysis.

KPI Cards with performance indicators (Gauge charts and target percentages).

Visual variety: Line Charts, Bar Charts, Waterfall Chart, Donut Chart for better insights.

Dynamic comparison of current performance vs goals for actionable decision-making.

## 🛠 Data Model
Built with a star schema to ensure optimal performance and flexibility:

Fact Table: Sales

Dimension Tables: Geography, Product, Sub-Categories, Categories, Date

Goal Tables: Total Revenue Goal, Total Units Goal, Gross Profit Goal

Additional Table: Last Refresh (to show dashboard update status)

## 📈 Insights Delivered
Identify top-performing products and categories by revenue.

Monitor MoM (Month-over-Month) and YoY (Year-over-Year) growth trends.

Track goal achievement percentage for profit, revenue, and units.

Highlight sales distribution across markets and product lines.
